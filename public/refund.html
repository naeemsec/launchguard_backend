Refund Policy

This policy explains how refund requests for LaunchGuard Pro subscriptions are handled. Please review it before purchasing a subscription.

1. Subscription cancellation

You may cancel your LaunchGuard Pro subscription through Paddle's secure Customer Portal, where available. Cancelling stops future subscription renewals according to the applicable billing terms. Cancellation does not automatically refund payments that have already been processed.

2. When a refund may be considered

Refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, including situations such as:

  • A duplicate subscription payment or duplicate charge.
  • An incorrect charge or billing amount.
  • A verified billing or service issue that materially affected the purchase.
  • A refund required by applicable consumer protection law or Paddle's applicable buyer terms.

A refund is not automatic merely because a subscription is cancelled or is no longer needed. Any decision will take account of the circumstances, applicable law and the terms governing the transaction.

3. How to request help or a refund review

Contact LaunchGuard support using the email below. Include the email address used at checkout, the approximate transaction date, and a short explanation of the issue. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or other sensitive payment credentials.

Payment processing and certain buyer-support processes are handled by Paddle. You may also use the receipt or Paddle Customer Portal to access the support options available for your transaction.

4. Processing and applicable rights

If a refund is approved, its processing and timing may depend on the original payment method and the payment processor. Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable rights or remedies you may have under applicable law or Paddle's applicable buyer terms.
